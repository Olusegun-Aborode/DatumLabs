## The month in one line

Lending demand rotated toward the majors while stablecoin borrow rates compressed. The interesting risk didn't sit in the headline TVL — it sat in the tail.

## Where the deposits went

Net new deposits concentrated in a handful of blue-chip markets. The long tail of smaller markets saw outflows — a flight-to-quality pattern we've seen repeat around volatility.

<Callout tone="brand" title="What we're watching">
Concentration cuts both ways: it lowers per-market operational risk but raises correlation. When one major re-prices, the others now follow faster than they did a year ago.
</Callout>

## Rates

Stablecoin borrow costs compressed across the board as utilization eased. Where variable rates stayed sticky, it was usually an incentive program holding utilization artificially high.

## The tail risk

The headline health of the system looked fine. Underneath, a cluster of positions collateralized by thinner assets drifted toward liquidation thresholds — the kind of exposure that only shows up when you model positions individually, not in aggregate.

Figures are pulled from Datum Labs archive nodes across 14 networks, normalized to a per-position model. "At-risk collateral" is collateral backing positions with a health factor below 1.1 at month end.
</Callout>
